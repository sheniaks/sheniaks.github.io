Two Ways to Say the Weather — the key grammar

1. Impersonal — no subject at all
Сегодня тепло.It's warm today.
Вчера было холодно.It was cold yesterday.
Завтра будет солнечно.It will be sunny tomorrow.
These end in -о. There's no "it" — just было / будет for past and future.
2. Noun + verb — for things that fall or blow
Идёт дождь.It's raining. (lit. "rain is going")
Шёл снег.It was snowing.
Дует ветер.The wind is blowing.
Rain and snow go in Russian — идёт. The verb agrees with the noun, like any sentence.
Mike already knows this! You wrote «недавно шёл дождь» — that's pattern 2, past tense, perfectly formed. And «сегодня облачно» is pattern 1. You were using both by instinct.
#EnglishRussianPronunciation
1 What's the weather like today? Какая сегодня погода? Ka-KA-ya see-VOHD-nyuh pa-GOH-da?
2 It's raining and the wind is blowing. Идёт дождь и дует ветер. Ee-DYOT dozhd' ee DOO-yet VYE-teer.
3 In the morning there was thick fog. Утром был густой туман. OOT-rum byl goos-TOY too-MAN.
4 A thunderstorm is coming. Надвигается гроза. Nad-vee-GA-ye-tsa gra-ZA.
5 It's very humid — hard to breathe. Очень влажно — трудно дышать. OH-chen' VLAZH-nuh — TROOD-nuh dy-SHAT'.
6 In winter in Winnipeg there's a hard frost. Зимой в Виннипеге сильный мороз. Zee-MOY v Vee-nee-PYE-gye SEEL'-nyy ma-ROS.
7 After the rain a rainbow appeared. После дождя появилась радуга. POS-lye dazh-DYA pa-ya-VEE-las' RA-doo-ga.
8 We were unlucky with the weather. Нам не повезло с погодой. Nam nye pa-veez-LOH s pa-GOH-duy.
Sentence 8 is yours: you wrote «Нам не очень повезло с погодой» — повезти (to be lucky) is an impersonal verb that takes the dative: мне повезло (I was lucky), нам не повезло (we were unlucky). Excellent, natural Russian.

#EnglishRussianPronunciation
W1 Because of the wildfires the air is smoky. Из-за пожаров воздух в дыму. Eez-za pa-ZHA-ruf VOZ-doo-h v dy-MOO.
W2 Today the air quality is poor. Сегодня плохое качество воздуха. See-VOHD-nyuh pla-HOH-ye KA-chest-vuh VOZ-doo-ha.
W3 The smoke smells of burning. Дым пахнет гарью. Dym PAH-nyet GAR'-yu.
W4 It's dangerous to breathe — better to stay home. Дышать опасно — лучше остаться дома. Dy-SHAT' a-PAS-nuh — LOOCH-she as-TA-tsa DOH-ma.
W5 Because of the smoke we didn't go for a walk. Из-за дыма мы не пошли гулять. Eez-za DY-ma my nye pash-LEE goo-LYAT'.
Grammar you already own: из-за (because of) takes the genitive — из-за пожаров, из-за дыма. You used exactly this in «из-за дождей города подтоплены». And пахнет гарью ("smells of burning") is the same instrumental you met in May with «воздух пах копотью». It all connects.

Погода меняется — Weather Over Time

8 min
Teacher: Weather is naturally about change and comparison — perfect for practising past/future and comparatives Mike already knows.
#EnglishRussianPronunciation
9 Yesterday it was hot, but today it's cooler. Вчера было жарко, а сегодня прохладнее. Vchee-RA BY-luh ZHAR-kuh, a see-VOHD-nyuh prah-HLAD-nye-ye.
10 The weather changes very quickly here. Здесь погода меняется очень быстро. Zdyes' pa-GOH-da mee-NYA-ye-tsa OH-chen' BYST-ruh.
11 What will the weather be tomorrow? Какая погода будет завтра? Ka-KA-ya pa-GOH-da BOO-deet ZAHF-truh?
12 They promise rain all week. Обещают дождь всю неделю. A-bee-SHCHA-yut dozhd' fsyu nee-DYEL-yu.
13 Because of the rain many cities are flooded. Из-за дождей многие города подтоплены. Eez-za dazh-DYEY MNOH-gee-ye ga-ra-DA pat-TOP-lee-ny.
14 Despite the rain, we go for a walk. Несмотря на дождь, мы идём гулять. Nees-mat-RYA na dozhd', my ee-DYOM goo-LYAT'.
Weather comparatives (built on what you know): тепло → теплее (warmer) · холодно → холоднее (colder) · жарко → жарче (hotter) · прохладно → прохладнее (cooler). Same -ее pattern as труднее.

Мини-диалог — Weather & the Trip

role-play
Teacher: Uses Mike's real trip to the US. He is addressed with вы. Read once, then swap roles and let Mike describe the weather on his actual vacation.
Олекс:
Майк, какая сегодня погода в Торонто?
Mayk, ka-KA-ya see-VOHD-nyuh pa-GOH-da v Ta-RON-tuh?
Mike, what's the weather like in Toronto today?
Майк:
Облачно и влажно. Утром шёл дождь, но сейчас дождя нет.
OH-bluch-nuh ee VLAZH-nuh. OOT-rum shol dozhd', no see-CHAS dazh-DYA nyet.
Cloudy and humid. It rained in the morning, but now there's no rain.
Олекс:
А какая погода была в отпуске, в Америке?
A ka-KA-ya pa-GOH-da by-LA v OT-poos-kye, v A-mee-ree-kye?
And what was the weather like on your vacation, in America?
Майк:
Нам не повезло с погодой. Часто шли дожди и была гроза.
Nam nye pa-veez-LOH s pa-GOH-duy. CHAS-tuh shlee dazh-DEE ee by-LA gra-ZA.
We were unlucky with the weather. It rained often and there was a thunderstorm.
Олекс:
Жаль! А в горах было холоднее?
Zhal'! A v ga-RAKH BY-luh hah-lad-NYE-ye?
A shame! And was it colder in the mountains?
Майк:
Да, в горах было прохладно, а в полях очень жарко.
Da, v ga-RAKH BY-luh prah-HLAD-nuh, a v pa-LYAKH OH-chen' ZHAR-kuh.
Yes, in the mountains it was cool, but in the fields it was very hot.
Олекс:
А Мабо? Он гулял, несмотря на дождь?
A MA-bo? On goo-LYAL, nees-mat-RYA na dozhd'?
And Mabo? Did he walk despite the rain?
Майк:
Конечно! Мабо любит гулять в любую погоду.
Ka-NYESH-nuh! MA-bo LYU-beet goo-LYAT' v lyu-BOO-yu pa-GOH-doo.
Of course! Mabo loves walking in any weather.
