«Когда встреча?» — Four Ways to Ask

10 min
Teacher: Mike tried four versions of the same question last week — all correct, each with a slightly different feel. Worth comparing side by side.
Когда у нас встреча? Kag-DA oo nas FSTRYE-cha? "our" meeting — shared
Когда мы встречаемся? Kag-DA my fstree-CHA-yem-sya? the action itself
Когда ваша встреча? Kag-DA VA-sha FSTRYE-cha? "your" meeting (formal)
Во сколько ваша встреча? Va SKOL'-kuh VA-sha FSTRYE-cha? asks for exact time
Когда = "when" (general). Во сколько = "at what time" (specific clock time) — more precise, like the difference between "when's the meeting?" and "what time exactly?" Both are natural; во сколько just narrows the answer.
